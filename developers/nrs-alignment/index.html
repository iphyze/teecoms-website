Scope of this review

This is a technical compatibility review, not evidence of NRS certification, a completed accreditation assessment, a signed SLA, production connectivity or successful regulator validation. The application-side findings include the 13 November 2026 Phase 5 backend/admin update, reviewed from the supplied source and offline test suite; they are not a claim about a newer production deployment. NRS requirements are based on the public official pages reviewed 13 November 2026. Items marked Gap, Planned or Requires verification are not implemented/verified assurances.

Official documentation and latest changes

The official documentation displays API v1.1. The published API changelog contains entries for April 2026, February 2026, December 2025, November 2025, October 2025 and April 2025. Its announced updates are described as optional for an initial three-month period after their respective release dates; integrators must check the latest effective requirements and official code lists before connecting. An additional cross-border schema is announced but its implementation timeline remains pending.

Payment and classification precision

The local JSON Schema accepting a value is not proof that the DigitalInvoice product can originate, manage, report and reconcile that transaction with NRS. Similarly, a now-implemented authenticated sync route does not mean current official data was actually fetched or cached on the deployed service. Local ready and submitted-like labels cannot be used as evidence of an NRS response.

The Phase 5 offline unit suites passed in the implementation delivery. No authenticated NRS sandbox response, accreditation, signature, production transaction or regulator acceptance was included. The rollout must remain fail-closed.

Mandatory fields and numerical consistency

The NRS v1.1 invoice schema identifies BusinessID, IRN, IssueDate, InvoiceTypeCode, InvoiceKind, document and tax currency, supplier party, tax total, legal monetary total and invoice lines as core invoice data. The DigitalInvoice local schema maps these with snake_case JSON fields. The official API, not this local JSON Schema, remains the acceptance authority.

Amounts are represented as decimal financial values and reconciled in integer kobo in the PHP adapter. NRS monetary totals require accurate line-extension, tax-exclusive, tax-inclusive and payable calculations. The existing downloadable examples remain fictional local fixtures, not NRS-validated examples; no stored database values or real customer details are exported.

The downloaded DigitalInvoice invoice schema, field mapping and sample payloads are local versioned artifacts. Do not replace them with invented fields or numbers simply to make them resemble an official response.

Service-level comparison and management decision

The NRS published SI/APP template lists 99.9% availability, 24/7 support, critical response 30 minutes / resolution 1 hour, high-priority response 1 hour / resolution 4 hours, and normal response 4 hours / resolution 8 hours. Its visible reference dates are 1 October 2024 (effective) and 10 October 2024 (updated). These dates belong to NRS's template, not Teecoms' agreement.

The current Teecoms SLA is unsigned and proposed and does not automatically inherit the NRS template figures. Management must decide whether Teecoms is applying as an SI, an APP or both; validate 24/7 on-call staffing, monitoring, backup/restore and business continuity evidence; set achievable service terms; and execute the final agreement. Existing draft terms remain explicitly unapproved until that process is complete.

Pre-production blockers and ownership

  1. Integration engineering: Complete the required B2G/G2B workflows and new tax/identification rules; run authenticated code-list sync with approved credentials; repair invoice classification and historic snapshots; design persistent collision-safe NRS invoice aliases; implement payment-update and response reconciliation.
  2. Signing and security: Assign SI/APP signing responsibilities, provision approved HSM/KMS custody, implement/test JAdES and certificate enrolment, and validate recovery/revocation. The Phase 5 public-certificate inspector is not a signer. Document completed VAPT only with signed evidence.
  3. Regulatory access and testing: Obtain authorised NRS sandbox and applicable service-provider credentials; test actual reference sync and B2B/B2C validation with approved test invoices; then sign/confirm/report, error/retry, IRN/CSID/QR and reconciliation scenarios before any independently reviewed production activation.
  4. Service operations: Compare the proposed SLA to the official service-provider reference, staff the agreed support model and obtain legal sign-off. Verify hosting/data region, encryption-in-transit and actual backup/restore performance from deployment evidence.
  5. Publishing: Confirm the exact production website/API origin, test all documentation downloads, and supply the NRS portal with public links only after the required approvals.
Dates and approval evidence

Document review date: 13 November 2026. A 2024 example date in the NRS template or fictional tests does not authorize backdating a Teecoms agreement, pretending a 2025 security audit happened, or listing an unapproved production service as active. The SLA's effective date must be the genuine date of execution/commencement.
