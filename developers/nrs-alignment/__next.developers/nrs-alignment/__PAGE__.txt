</div></aside><h2 id="mandatory-fields-and-numerical-consistency">Mandatory fields and numerical consistency</h2><p>The <a href="https://einvoice.nrs.gov.ng/docs/system-integrator/invoice-schema?version=1.1">NRS v1.1 invoice schema</a> identifies <code>BusinessID</code>, <code>IRN</code>, <code>IssueDate</code>, <code>InvoiceTypeCode</code>, <code>InvoiceKind</code>, document and tax currency, supplier party, tax total, legal monetary total and invoice lines as core invoice data. The DigitalInvoice local schema maps these with <strong>snake_case JSON</strong> fields. The official API, not this local JSON Schema, remains the acceptance authority.</p>
<p>Amounts are represented as decimal financial values and reconciled in integer kobo in the PHP adapter. NRS monetary totals require accurate line-extension, tax-exclusive, tax-inclusive and payable calculations. The existing downloadable examples remain <strong>fictional local fixtures</strong>, not NRS-validated examples; no stored database values or real customer details are exported.</p>
<p>The downloaded <a href="/docs/digitalinvoice-invoice-schema-v1.0.json">DigitalInvoice invoice schema</a>, <a href="/docs/digitalinvoice-field-mapping-v1.0.csv">field mapping</a> and <a href="/developers/data-structure/">sample payloads</a> are local versioned artifacts. <strong>Do not replace them with invented fields or numbers simply to make them resemble an official response.</strong></p>
<h2 id="service-level-comparison-and-management-decision">Service-level comparison and management decision</h2><p>The <a href="https://einvoice.nrs.gov.ng/serivce-level-agreement">NRS published SI/APP template</a> lists <strong>99.9% availability</strong>, <strong>24/7 support</strong>, critical response <strong>30 minutes</strong> / resolution <strong>1 hour</strong>, high-priority response <strong>1 hour</strong> / resolution <strong>4 hours</strong>, and normal response <strong>4 hours</strong> / resolution <strong>8 hours</strong>. Its visible reference dates are <strong>1 October 2024</strong> (effective) and <strong>10 October 2024</strong> (updated). These dates belong <strong>to NRS&#39;s template</strong>, not Teecoms&#39; agreement.</p>
<p>The current <a href="/sla/">Teecoms SLA</a> is <strong>unsigned and proposed</strong> and does <strong>not</strong> automatically inherit the NRS template figures. Management must decide whether Teecoms is applying as an SI, an APP or both; validate 24/7 on-call staffing, monitoring, backup/restore and business continuity evidence; set achievable service terms; and execute the final agreement. Existing draft terms remain explicitly unapproved until that process is complete.</p>
<h2 id="pre-production-blockers-and-ownership">Pre-production blockers and ownership</h2><ol>
<li><strong>Integration engineering:</strong> Complete the required B2G/G2B workflows and new tax/identification rules; run authenticated code-list sync with approved credentials; repair invoice classification and historic snapshots; design persistent collision-safe NRS invoice aliases; implement payment-update and response reconciliation.</li>
<li><strong>Signing and security:</strong> Assign SI/APP signing responsibilities, provision approved HSM/KMS custody, implement/test JAdES and certificate enrolment, and validate recovery/revocation. The Phase 5 public-certificate inspector is not a signer. Document completed VAPT only with signed evidence.</li>
<li><strong>Regulatory access and testing:</strong> Obtain authorised NRS sandbox and applicable service-provider credentials; test actual reference sync and B2B/B2C validation with approved test invoices; then sign/confirm/report, error/retry, IRN/CSID/QR and reconciliation scenarios before any independently reviewed production activation.</li>
<li><strong>Service operations:</strong> Compare the proposed SLA to the official service-provider reference, staff the agreed support model and obtain legal sign-off. Verify hosting/data region, encryption-in-transit and actual backup/restore performance from deployment evidence.</li>
<li><strong>Publishing:</strong> Confirm the exact production website/API origin, test all <a href="/developers/">documentation downloads</a>, and supply the NRS portal with public links only after the required approvals.</li>
</ol>
